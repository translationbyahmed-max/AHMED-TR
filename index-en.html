About Us

I am Ahmed, a researcher and enthusiast in languages and translation, with a special focus on Video Game Localization. I founded the AHMED-TR project as a non-profit initiative aimed at bringing the global gaming experience to Arab players in sound Arabic that respects cultural context and accurate terminology, while preserving the spirit of the original work.
